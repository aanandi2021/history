    Getting around

    Hover the timeline to move through time. Click an empty spot to pin the date there, and click again to let go.

    Click any event, ruler, chapter or book to open its card and links. Chapters (the band at the top of the timeline) zoom the map and timeline together. Big parts hold smaller chapters: click ▾ to fold a part and ▸ to open it. Zoomed out, the map shows a part's overview; zoom in and its chapters' maps take over. The Key box on the map says who each colour is.

    Drag the timeline to pan. Ctrl + wheel (or pinch) zooms it. The map zooms with the wheel.

    Keys: ← → previous / next event · Space play · / search · Esc clear.

    The address bar always holds the current period, date and selection, so you can paste it into an Obsidian note.
